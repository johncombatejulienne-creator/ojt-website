## 📝 Registration & Setup

### Step 1: Sign In with Gmail

1. Go to **https://immersion-tracker.vercel.app/login**
2. Click the **"Student"** button
3. Click **"Sign in with Google"**
4. Choose your Gmail account
5. Allow access when asked

### Step 2: Enter Your Student ID

After signing in, you'll be asked to enter:
- 📋 **Student ID** (e.g., 2026-00123)

### Step 3: Select Your Strand & Section

You'll need to choose:
- 📚 **Grade Level**: 12
- 🎓 **Strand**: ICT, STEM, ABM, or HUMSS
- 📖 **Section**: 12-A or 12-B

**Example:**
```
Grade Level: 12
Strand: ICT
Section: 12-A
Company: Tech Solutions Inc.
```

### Step 4: Automatic Teacher Assignment

The system will **automatically assign you** to the correct teacher based on your strand and section. You don't need to choose your teacher!

**You'll see a message like:**
> "Successfully registered! You've been assigned to Ms. Sarah Johnson."

## 📝 Submitting Daily Narratives

### When to Submit

Submit your narrative **every work day** before **11:59 PM**.

⏰ **On Time** = Submitted on the same day
🚫 **Late** = Submitted after the day is over

### What to Include

**Required Information:**
1. **Date** - The work day date
2. **Time In** - When you started (e.g., 8:00 AM)
3. **Time Out** - When you finished (e.g., 5:00 PM)
4. **Hours Rendered** - Auto-calculated (e.g., 8 hours)
5. **Narrative** - Detailed description of your day
6. **Photos** - Pictures from your workplace

### Writing a Good Narrative

**✅ Good Example:**
```
Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM):
I started my day by attending the team meeting where we discussed 
the project progress. I was assigned to help with testing the new 
website features. I learned how to use the browser developer tools 
to check for bugs. My supervisor, Mr. Cruz, taught me about 
responsive design and how websites should work on different devices.

Afternoon (1:00 PM - 5:00 PM):
After lunch, I continued testing and found 3 bugs which I reported 
to the development team. I also helped organize the documentation 
for the project. I learned about version control using Git and 
created my first commit.

What I Learned:
- How to test website responsiveness
- Using browser developer tools
- Basic Git commands
- Importance of proper documentation
```

**❌ Bad Example:**
```
I worked today. I did my tasks. It was okay.
```

### Tips for Writing

✅ **Do:**
- Be specific about tasks
- Mention what you learned
- Include supervisor names
- Describe skills practiced
- Reflect on experiences

❌ **Don't:**
- Write one-sentence narratives
- Copy from previous days
- Be too vague
- Forget important details

## 📸 Taking & Uploading Photos

### Why Photos Are Important

Photos prove:
- ✅ You were at the workplace
- ✅ You did actual work
- ✅ You're learning real skills

### How to Take Photos

**Using Your Phone:**
1. Open the narrative submission form
2. Click **"Open Camera"**
3. Allow camera access
4. Take photos of your workplace/activities
5. Click **"Capture Photo"**
6. Review and submit

**Important:**
- 📸 Take photos **on the actual work day**
- 📍 Take them **at your workplace**
- 🚫 Don't use old photos from previous days
- 🚫 Don't edit or modify photos

### What to Photograph

✅ **Good Photo Subjects:**
- Your work station
- Tasks you're performing
- Projects you're working on
- Team meetings (with permission)
- Your supervisor (with permission)
- Workplace environment

❌ **Don't Photograph:**
- Confidential documents
- Computer screens with sensitive data
- Other people without permission
- Prohibited areas

### Photo Verification

The system **automatically checks**:
- ✅ When the photo was taken
- ✅ What device was used
- ✅ Location (if available)

**If verification fails**, your teacher will review manually.

## 🔍 Understanding Verification

### What is Verification?

The system checks if your submissions are:
- ⏰ **On Time** - Submitted on the correct day
- 📸 **Authentic** - Photos are from the actual date
- 📍 **Valid** - Taken at the correct location

### On-Time vs Late

**On Time** ✅
- Submitted before 11:59 PM on the work day
- Shows green checkmark
- Counts toward completion

**Late** 🚫
- Submitted after the day is over
- Shows red indicator
- May not count (check with teacher)

### Photo Verification

Each photo is checked for:

✅ **Capture Timestamp**
- When was it taken?
- Is it from today?
- Is it too old?

✅ **Device Information**
- What device was used?
- Is metadata intact?

✅ **Location** (if available)
- GPS coordinates
- Distance from workplace

**Verification Statuses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ✅ Verified | Photo passed all checks |
| ⚠️ Warning | Minor issue detected |
| 🚫 Failed | Verification failed |
| ⏳ Pending Review | Teacher will check manually |

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Late Submissions
**Problem:** Waiting until Sunday to submit all week's narratives

**Solution:** Submit every day before midnight

### Mistake 2: Using Old Photos
**Problem:** Using photos from previous weeks

**Solution:** Take fresh photos each work day

### Mistake 3: Vague Narratives
**Problem:** "I worked today. I did tasks. It was good."

**Solution:** Write detailed descriptions with specific tasks and learnings

### Mistake 4: Missing Information
**Problem:** Forgetting to record time in/out

**Solution:** Double-check all required fields before submitting

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### I Can't Log In
- ✅ Use the Gmail account you registered with
- ✅ Check your internet connection
- ✅ Try a different browser
- ✅ Contact your teacher

### I Can't Upload Photos
- ✅ Allow camera access in browser settings
- ✅ Check file size (max 5MB per photo)
- ✅ Use supported formats (JPG, PNG, HEIC)
- ✅ Try a different device

### My Submission Shows "Late"
- ✅ Check your device clock
- ✅ Submit before 11:59 PM
- ✅ If incorrect, contact your teacher

### Photo Verification Failed
- ✅ Make sure photo is from today
- ✅ Don't edit or modify photos
- ✅ Take with original camera app
- ✅ Teacher will review manually
